Plays anime openings and endings for your shows. Put theme files in a folder and the plugin matches them to your shows and seasons, then hands them to clients that support it (for example AsterFIN). Jellyfin's own theme music and video are not affected.

Themes folder

Make one folder per show, named exactly like the show's folder in your library, and put the files inside: OP1.webm, OP1.ogg, ED2.webm. AnimeThemes file names such as SousouNoFrieren-OP1.webm work too. To limit a theme to one season, use a subfolder like Season 2, or anidb-18886 to target one AniDB entry. Keep this folder outside your libraries so they don't treat the themes as ordinary videos.

Metadata

Song titles, artists and episode lists come from AnimeThemes.moe (not affiliated), using the AniDB IDs on your seasons (Shokofin sets these). To go easy on their server, requests are batched, answers are cached, and lookups pause for a day if they refuse us. Untick this to never contact them. You can still fill in details by hand in the editor below.
Applies to anime AnimeThemes has themes for. Ones it has nothing for are asked again sooner (after 1, 3 and 7 days, then every 30), since they are usually just not added yet.
For a show with themes but no AniDB, AniList, MyAnimeList or Kitsu ID, search AnimeThemes by the show's name. This sends the show names to them, ten to a request, and the answers are cached. It never replaces an ID. Each theme in the editor says when it was matched this way, and you can correct it there.
Higher is stricter. At 80, small differences in spelling or punctuation still match, but a different show does not. Raise it if you see wrong matches.

Jellyfin libraries

Two ordinary libraries, kept up to date after every scan: a Music Videos library of the theme videos and a Music library of their songs, an album per show. Playlists, favourites, watched state, search and parental controls then work as for anything else in Jellyfin, and apps show them like any library. The videos are links to the playable copies, so they take no extra space; the songs are small copies with their details written inside.
The plugin fills this folder itself, with Videos and Songs inside. It must be outside the themes folder and every other library.
Given to videos marked NSFW, so users whose parental controls stop at a lower rating don't see them. Spoiler and NSFW themes are also tagged "Spoiler" and "NSFW".

Review clips

Missing an OP or ED? The plugin can cut it out of your own episodes, using chapters named OP/Opening or ED/Ending (for endings, also Credits, End Credits, Outro or Closing). Each clip waits below for your review and isn't served until you approve it. Approving moves it into the themes folder; then use Add to library to index it and fetch song details. This also runs as a scheduled task, Cut OP/ED clips from chapters.
Click a clip to select it, then A approve, R reject, T reject and try another episode, P preview, ↑/↓ move.
