Requests (Seerr)

Aster's request features (requesting a film or show, and seeing a request's progress) stay off unless they're turned on here. Each person signs in to Seerr with their own Jellyfin login, so no Seerr key is needed.

Tracking

What each person watches, in any app, is added to the history of the services they link: a film or episode once it's stopped past 85%, or marked played. People link their own accounts from Aster's settings, or here for anyone. Simkl now; Trakt, AniList, MyAnimeList, Last.fm, ListenBrainz and Hardcover follow.
