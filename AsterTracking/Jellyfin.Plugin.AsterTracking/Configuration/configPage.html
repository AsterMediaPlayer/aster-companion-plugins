Requests (Seerr)

Aster's request features (requesting a film or show, and seeing a request's progress) stay off unless they're turned on here. Nobody signs in to Seerr in the apps: this server talks to Seerr for them, as each person's own Seerr user, so Seerr's permissions, quotas and auto-approval still apply. Someone without a Seerr account gets one from Jellyfin the first time they use requests, with Seerr's defaults for new users.

Where this server reaches Seerr: its local address is best. The apps never use it.
From Seerr: Settings › General › API Key.

Tracking

What each person watches, in any app, is sent to the services they link, once a film or episode is stopped past 85% or marked played: Simkl adds it to their history, and AniList and MyAnimeList move an anime's progress on. Music goes to Last.fm and ListenBrainz once a song has played half way or four minutes, and audiobook progress to Hardcover. People link their own accounts from Aster's settings, or here for anyone. Trakt follows.

Discover

Your own key from themoviedb.org (Settings › API: free for personal use). Aster's Discover uses it for films and shows like the ones each person watches, and for what's trending. Paste the API Read Access Token or the API Key. Without one, Discover still shows anime recommendations, your server's unwatched picks, and everyone's linked lists.

Kept out of Discover

Titles with any of these never show in Aster's Discover, in the server's rows or in Seerr's rows and search, and their Seerr pages won't open. Genres and tags are matched by name, ignoring case: AniList's and MyAnimeList's genres and tags, TMDB's genres and keywords, and your library's genres and tags. Seerr's own rows and search can only be checked by genre and the adult flag; keywords count there once a title's page opens. Separate names with commas or new lines.
