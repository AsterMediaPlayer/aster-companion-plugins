# About these screenshots

The four images in this folder (`menu-editor.jpg`, `title-page.jpg`, `main-menu.jpg`,
`special-features.jpg`) are screenshots of this plugin running on a Jellyfin server, with a menu built for
the film *Thor: Ragnarok*.

**They are not covered by this project's GPL-3.0 licence.** They contain material that belongs to others:
film stills and promotional images (originally from Marvel Studios / Disney, shown here via The Movie
Database), the film's poster and title logo, and the artwork of the film's Blu-ray menu, which the menu is
modelled on. They are included **only to illustrate what the plugin does**, in documentation, and are not
part of the plugin, its examples or its assets: the plugin's own files contain no studio artwork.

If you are a rights holder and would like any of these removed, please open an issue and they will be
taken down.

**If you contribute screenshots,** please make them from the bundled example menus (which use art made for
this project) rather than from a real film, so the documentation stays free of other people's artwork.
